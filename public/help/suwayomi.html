Connect your Mihon sources

Suwayomi runs Mihon-compatible Android extensions on your computer or server. This PWA keeps your library, reading progress, and downloaded pages on your iPhone.

Start the included server

  1. On a computer with Docker, open the PWA repository. Copy .env.example to .env and choose SUWAYOMI_PASSWORD.
  2. Run docker compose up --build -d. Open http://localhost:4567 on that computer and sign in with username reader and your chosen password.
  3. In Suwayomi’s WebUI, add an extension repository you trust and install the sources used by your Mihon library.
  4. In this app’s Connections, enter those credentials and choose Use bundled server.

For an existing server, enter its HTTPS URL instead. Basic authentication is supported. For authenticated backup uploads, use the bundled proxy or restore the original file through Suwayomi’s WebUI.

Install on your iPhone

Serve the PWA over trusted HTTPS. For the included Caddy setup, point a domain to your Docker host, set MIHON_DOMAIN in .env, and run:

docker compose --profile https up --build -d

Open your HTTPS domain in Safari, then choose Share → Add to Home Screen. Use your installed app for both importing and reading so its data stays in the same storage.

Move your library and downloads

  1. In More → Backup & restore, choose your .tachibk file, review the import, and restore it.
  2. In Connections, choose Copy to server beside the retained backup. Refresh your connected sources after installing the matching extensions.
  3. Open a title and download chapters. Keep the app visible while the queue runs; completed chapters work in airplane mode.
  4. To move existing Android downloads, put each chapter’s numbered images in a ZIP/CBZ file. Choose that imported title as the destination under Import offline chapters. Keep exact chapter names when numbers are ambiguous.

A Mihon backup contains metadata and progress; its chapter images are separate. Export this PWA’s library as JSON and save its downloaded chapters as CBZ from Downloads when moving devices.

iOS can suspend background downloads and evict browser storage. Request persistent storage in Settings, keep enough free space, and retain your exports.
