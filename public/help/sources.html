Built-in sources

Open Browse → Sources and select MangaHub, Mangakakalot, Manhwa18.cc, MangaDex, Asura Scans, MangaLife, MangaFox, or Kissmanga.in. These sources are included in the app and require no extension installation or Suwayomi connection.

Restore your Mihon backup to keep your original title links and reading progress. Open a title and refresh chapters, then download the chapters you want available offline.

Mangakakalot uses its working official mangakakalove.com mirror for Browse and search. Migration now searches when you select a replacement source and preserves matching progress, categories and history.

A source can block app requests, remove a title or change its website. MangaHub still denies requests after the normal Mihon key-refresh flow. Safari website cookies cannot be shared automatically with the PWA's proxy.

During live verification, MangaHub and Kissmanga.in blocked requests; Manhwa18.cc had an untrusted certificate and MangaLife's original domain failed DNS. Asura Scans, Mangakakalot and MangaFox returned readable chapter images. Every backup source has its bundled extension logo.

Downloaded chapters stay readable offline. Export a new .tachibk under More → Backup & restore to bring your current progress back to Android. Export downloaded images separately as CBZ.
