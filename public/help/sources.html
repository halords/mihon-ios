Built-in sources

Open Browse → Sources and select MangaHub, Mangakakalot, Manhwa18.cc, MangaDex, Asura Scans, MangaLife, MangaFox, or Kissmanga.in. These sources are included in the app and require no extension installation or Suwayomi connection.

Restore your Mihon backup to keep your original title links and reading progress. Open a title and refresh chapters, then download the chapters you want available offline.

A source can block app requests, remove a title or change its website. The app shows these failures when they occur. During verification, MangaHub and Kissmanga.in blocked requests; Manhwa18.cc and MangaLife were unavailable from the test host. Asura Scans, Mangakakalot and MangaFox returned readable chapter images.

Downloaded chapters stay readable offline. Export a new .tachibk under More → Backup & restore to bring your current progress back to Android. Export downloaded images separately as CBZ.
